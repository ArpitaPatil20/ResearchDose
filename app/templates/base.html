Install ResearchDose on iPhone / iPad

To install ResearchDose on iOS Safari without an App Store download:

  1. Tap the Share button in Safari's bottom toolbar.
  2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen .
  3. Tap Add at top right. The ResearchDose icon will appear on your home screen.
Once installed, it opens instantly in full-screen and works offline!
Install ResearchDose on Android

To install ResearchDose directly on your Android phone:

  1. Tap the three dots menu () in the top-right corner of Chrome or your browser.
  2. Select Install app or Add to Home screen.
  3. Tap Install when prompted. The app icon will appear on your home screen.
Launches in fullscreen mode and runs calculations 100% offline!
