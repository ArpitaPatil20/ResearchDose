Body Surface Area (BSA) Normalization Principle

Direct mg/kg extrapolation between small animals and humans causes severe overdosing due to differences in surface-to-mass ratio and basal metabolic clearance. ResearchDose standardizes on the validated FDA (2005) $K_m$ normalization equation:

Target Dose (mg/kg) = Source Dose (mg/kg) × [ Km(source) / Km(target) ]
Where: Km = Body Weight (kg) / Body Surface Area (m²)
Total Dose (mg) = Target Dose (mg/kg) × Target Body Weight (kg)
Core Integrity Rules:
  • Human to Animal: Reverses $K_m$ factor ratio symmetrically without inventing ad-hoc constants.
  • Concentration to Volume: Volume (mL) = Total Dose (mg) / Formulation Concentration (mg/mL).
  • Safety Margin: Human clinical trials require an additional safety factor ($\ge 10\times$) applied to animal NOAEL doses.
