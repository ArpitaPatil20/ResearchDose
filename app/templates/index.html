Mathematical Principles

Interspecies scaling is based on Body Surface Area (BSA) allometry because basal metabolic rates are proportional to surface area rather than body mass.

Target Dose (mg/kg) = Source Dose × (Km_source / Km_target)
Total Dose (mg) = Dose (mg/kg) × Subject Weight (kg)
